# Contributing to The Oven

Thanks for wanting to add a game! The full rules live on the site:
**https://sakhadib.github.io/games/contributing.html** (source: [`contributing.html`](contributing.html)).

The short version:

- One game, one folder (`lowercase-with-dashes/index.html`), ideally one self-contained file.
- Your own work. No ripped sprites, music, characters, or brand names.
- No ads, no trackers, no logins, no data leaving the player's device (only CDN libraries, Google Fonts, and the site's GoatCounter tag are allowed).
- Keyboard controls on desktop, touch controls if it can work on phones.
- Add the SEO head tags, the analytics tag, a 1200×630 screenshot in `assets/shots/`, a card at the end of the shelf in `index.html`, a `hasPart` entry in its JSON-LD, a line in `sitemap.xml`, and a row in the README table.
- Open a PR titled `Add game: Your Game` with a pitch, how to play, a screenshot, and what you tested on.
